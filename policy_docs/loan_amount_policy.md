# Loan Amount & LTV Policy

## Purpose
This document defines the maximum loan amounts, loan-to-value (LTV) limits, and
loan sizing rules for all products offered by Apex Lending Group.

---

## 1. Maximum Loan Amounts by Product

| Product                   | Minimum Loan | Maximum Loan  | Notes                                    |
|---------------------------|-------------|----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Conventional Mortgage     | $50,000     | $726,200       | Conforming limit (2024 FHFA baseline)    |
| Jumbo Mortgage            | $726,201    | $3,000,000     | Requires 20% down; FICO ≥ 700           |
| FHA Mortgage              | $50,000     | $472,030       | Per FHA national floor (2024)            |

## 2. Loan-to-Value (LTV) Requirements

### 2.1 Conventional Mortgage
- **LTV ≤ 80%**: No private mortgage insurance (PMI) required.
- **LTV 80.01% – 95%**: PMI required; max CLTV (combined LTV) = 95%.
- **LTV > 95%**: Not permitted.

### 2.2 FHA Mortgage
- **Minimum down payment**: 3.5% (FICO ≥ 580) or 10% (FICO 500–579).
- **Maximum LTV**: 96.5%.
- MIP (mortgage insurance premium) is required for the life of the loan if LTV > 90% at origination.

### 2.3 Jumbo Mortgage
- **Maximum LTV**: 80% ($726,201 – $1,500,000); 75% ($1,500,001 – $3,000,000).
- Reserves: ≥ 12 months PITI at closing.

### 2.4 Auto Loan
- **Maximum LTV**: 110% of the vehicle's NADA/KBB clean retail value for prime borrowers.
- **Subprime (FICO < 620)**: Maximum LTV 90%.

## 3. Loan Sizing Rules

### 3.1 Payment-to-Income (PTI) Ratio — Auto Loans
- Monthly vehicle payment must not exceed **15%** of gross monthly income.
- Exceptions require manager approval and a back-end DTI ≤ 40%.

### 3.2 Loan Amount Relative to Income — Personal Loans
- Maximum loan amount = **5× gross annual income**.
- Example: an applicant earning $50,000/year may not borrow more than $250,000
  (subject to product maximum of $100,000).

### 3.3 Large Loan Flag
- Any personal loan request > $50,000 or any mortgage > $600,000 triggers an
  automatic second-reviewer flag in the underwriting system.

## 4. Down Payment Sources

Acceptable sources of down payment funds:
- Personal savings (bank statements required — 60-day history)
- Gift funds from family members (gift letter required; must be documented as a gift, not a loan)
- Employer assistance programs
- Government or non-profit down-payment assistance programs (approved list maintained by Compliance)

**Unacceptable sources:**
- Borrowed funds (personal loans, credit card advances) used as down payment — automatic decline.
- Undocumented cash deposits > $1,000 within 60 days of application — must be sourced.

---

## 5. Refinance Guidelines

### 5.1 Rate-and-Term Refinance
- Maximum LTV: 97% (Fannie Mae) / 96.5% (FHA).
- Minimum seasoning: 6 months of payments on current mortgage.

### 5.2 Cash-Out Refinance
- **Maximum LTV**: 80% for conventional; 85% for FHA.
- **Minimum equity**: $50,000 must remain after cash-out.
- Cash-out amount is limited to the lesser of: (a) actual equity withdrawal, or
  (b) documented purpose (home improvement quotes, debt payoff statements, etc.).

---

## 6. High-Cost Loan Thresholds (HPML / HOEPA)

Loans exceeding the following APR thresholds are classified as Higher-Priced Mortgage Loans
(HPML) and require additional disclosures and a mandatory escrow account:
- First lien: APR > APOR + 1.5%
- Subordinate lien: APR > APOR + 3.5%

Loans exceeding HOEPA thresholds require additional consumer protections and senior
underwriter sign-off.

## 7. Geographic Restrictions

- Apex Lending Group is not licensed to originate loans in the following states: North Dakota.
- Properties in FEMA-designated Special Flood Hazard Areas (SFHA) require flood insurance
  as a condition of approval.
