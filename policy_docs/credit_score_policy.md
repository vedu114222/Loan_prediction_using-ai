# Credit Score Policy

## Purpose
This document defines the minimum credit score requirements and associated underwriting
rules for all personal and mortgage loan products offered by Apex Lending Group.

---

## 1. Credit Score Tiers

| Tier         | FICO Range | Treatment                                       |
|--------------|------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| **Prime**    | 750 – 850  | Automatic approval consideration; best rates    |
| **Near-Prime**| 680 – 749  | Standard underwriting review                    |
| **Subprime** | 620 – 679  | Manual review required; higher interest rates   |
| **Deep Subprime** | 580 – 619 | Approval only with compensating factors     |
| **Decline**  | < 580      | Application declined; reapply after 6 months    |

---

## 2. Minimum Score Requirements by Product

### 2.1 Conventional Mortgage
- **Minimum FICO**: 640
- Applicants below 640 are ineligible unless accompanied by a co-borrower with FICO ≥ 720.

### 2.2 Personal / Unsecured Loan
- **Minimum FICO**: 620
- Applicants in the 620–659 range must demonstrate at least 24 months of stable employment
  and a debt-to-income (DTI) ratio below 36%.

### 2.3 Auto Loan
- **Minimum FICO**: 580
- Applicants below 620 are subject to a maximum loan-to-value (LTV) ratio of 90% and
  require a minimum down payment of 10%.

### 2.4 Small Business Loan
- **Minimum FICO (personal guarantee)**: 650
- Business must have been operational for ≥ 24 months.

---

## 3. Compensating Factors

When an applicant falls below the standard FICO threshold, underwriters **may** approve the
application if **two or more** of the following compensating factors are present:

1. Verified liquid assets ≥ 6 months of projected mortgage/loan payments.
2. Demonstrated income growth of ≥ 10% over the past 24 months.
3. Low DTI ratio (< 28%) despite the lower credit score.
4. A co-applicant with a prime-tier credit score (≥ 750).
5. No derogatory marks (collections, charge-offs, judgments) in the past 36 months.

---

## 4. Credit Inquiries

- Hard inquiries within the past 12 months are reviewed; more than **6 hard inquiries**
  within 12 months triggers a manual-review flag.
- Rate-shopping inquiries (multiple mortgage inquiries within 45 days) are treated as
  a single inquiry per FICO scoring conventions.

---

## 5. Derogatory Credit Events — Waiting Periods

| Event                    | Waiting Period Before Eligibility |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Bankruptcy (Chapter 7)   | 4 years from discharge            |
| Bankruptcy (Chapter 13)  | 2 years from discharge            |
| Foreclosure              | 7 years                           |
| Short sale               | 4 years                           |
| Collections (unpaid)     | Must be paid and seasoned ≥ 12 mo.|

---

## 6. Credit Score Recalculation

Applicants whose score is within 20 points of a tier boundary may request a "rapid
rescore" from the lender. The rescore typically takes 3–5 business days.
