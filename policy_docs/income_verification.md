# Income Verification Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes the income documentation, verification standards, and
debt-to-income (DTI) ratio limits used by Apex Lending Group to assess an
applicant's capacity to repay a loan.

---

## 1. Acceptable Income Sources

### 1.1 Primary Employment (W-2)
- Provide the two most recent **W-2 forms** and the two most recent **pay stubs**.
- Applicants must have been employed at their current position for ≥ **6 months**,
  unless they are in the same field with continuous employment ≥ **2 years**.
- Seasonal or temporary employment requires a 2-year history in the same field.

### 1.2 Self-Employment / 1099 Income
- Provide the two most recent **federal tax returns** (personal and business if applicable).
- Business must have been operational for ≥ **2 years**.
- Qualifying income is calculated as the **lesser** of the two-year average or the most
  recent year's net income, adjusted for depreciation and business use of home.
- Year-over-year income decline of > **25%** requires a written explanation and may
  result in a lower qualifying income.

### 1.3 Social Security / Disability / Pension
- Provide the most recent **award letter** or benefit statement.
- Income may be **grossed up** by 125% if it is non-taxable.

### 1.4 Rental Income
- Provide the most recent **federal tax return** (Schedule E).
- Qualifying rental income = 75% of gross rents (to account for vacancy and maintenance).
- Property must have been rented for ≥ **12 months** or supported by executed lease
  agreements.

### 1.5 Investment / Dividend Income
- Provide the two most recent years of **federal tax returns** and brokerage statements.
- Income must be demonstrated for ≥ **2 years** and expected to continue for ≥ 3 years.

### 1.6 Child Support / Alimony
- Provide the divorce decree or separation agreement **and** 12 months of bank statements
  confirming consistent receipt.
- Must continue for ≥ **36 months** from application date.

---

## 2. Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio

### 2.1 Calculation

```
Front-end DTI = Monthly Housing Expense ÷ Gross Monthly Income
Back-end DTI  = (Total Monthly Debt Payments) ÷ Gross Monthly Income
```

Monthly debt payments include: housing PITI, student loans, car loans, credit card
minimums, personal loans, and child support obligations.

### 2.2 Limits

| Product                   | Max Front-End DTI | Max Back-End DTI |
|---------------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Conventional Mortgage     | 28%               | 43%              |
| FHA Mortgage (approved)   | 31%               | 50%              |
| Personal / Unsecured Loan | N/A               | 40%              |
| Auto Loan                 | N/A               | 45%              |
| Small Business Loan       | N/A               | 38%              |

### 2.3 Compensating Factors for Elevated DTI
If back-end DTI exceeds the limit by ≤ 5 percentage points, approval may still be
considered if **two** compensating factors are present:
- Credit score ≥ 720
- Verified reserves ≥ 12 months PITI
- Documented income increase of ≥ 10% expected within 60 days (offer letter)
- LTV ≤ 80%

---

## 3. Minimum Income Thresholds

| Product                   | Minimum Annual Gross Income |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Conventional Mortgage     | $35,000                     |
| Personal Loan             | $25,000                     |
| Auto Loan                 | $20,000                     |
| Small Business Loan       | $50,000 (combined)          |

Applicants below these thresholds are automatically declined regardless of other factors.

---

## 4. Income Continuity Requirements

- Income from any source must be **likely to continue** for at least **3 years** after
  the loan closing date.
- If an applicant is within 3 years of retirement age and retirement income has not yet
  been established, the underwriter must verify retirement assets sufficient to support
  ongoing payments.

---

## 5. Employment Gap Policy

- Gaps of ≤ **30 days** between jobs: acceptable with a letter of explanation (LOE).
- Gaps of **31–180 days**: acceptable if the applicant has ≥ 2 years in the same
  field and the gap was due to documented reasons (medical, layoff).
- Gaps > **180 days** within the past 24 months: require senior underwriter approval.

---

## 6. Verbal Verification of Employment (VVOE)

A VVOE must be completed within **10 business days** of closing for all W-2 borrowers.
Self-employed borrowers must provide a CPA letter or recent business license instead.
